# Alpine Star - brand foundation
Purpose: Make the preparation as intentional as the climb. Vision: More mountain journeys approached with confidence, knowledge, and respect. Mission: Build a planning companion bringing routes, conditions, and preparation together for mountaineers.
Audience: Aspiring and experienced mountaineers planning serious alpine objectives. Positioning: An expedition journal's inspiration with field equipment's clarity.
Values: Respect the mountain; prepare deliberately; make uncertainty visible; keep the team informed. Personality: Awe-inspired, composed, capable, encouraging. Promise: A clearer picture of the climb ahead. Never promise a guaranteed safe summit or functioning concept-stage features.

## Identity
Name: Alpine Star, preserve spelling. Custom outlined twin-summit mark with small four-point star. Forest #193B30, ivory #F4F3EC, ink #172A24, orange #F27645, sage #738578, contour #D8DDD0. Oversized editorial serif headlines (Georgia fallback), clean sans-serif body, monospace expedition labels. Horizontal wordmark minimum 120px; one star-width clear space. Ivory on dark; forest on light.
Photography: cinematic natural alpine landscapes, crisp snow, blue-grey rock, warm sunrise, appropriately equipped climbers. Generate with Higgsfield. Preserve image subjects and summit focal points.

## Voice and copy
Tagline: Every summit starts long before the climb.
Hero: A higher calling. A clearer way up.
Intro: Meet the mountain with more than ambition. Your routes, preparation, and mountain conditions - together in one companion.
CTA: Join the waitlist. Support: Built for the climb. Coming soon.
Product: Your next summit. A little more within reach.
Closing: The mountains are calling. Be ready when you answer.
Planned capabilities: Satellite-connected weather; preparation checklists; offline maps; live risk assessment; altitude, oxygen availability, and mountain conditions. Do not imply a phone measures blood oxygen. Map values are illustrative. No invented reviews, users, partners, or validation statistics.

## Brand protection
Review copy as functionality evolves, preserving concept-stage clarity. Keep name, mark, palette, and tone consistent. Brand ownership/name clearance is a future pre-launch task; do not claim trademark status. Retain generated asset provenance and audit unsupported safety claims before publication.
